<p style="text-align:left;">Our work combines computational modelling, neuroimaging and psychopharmacology, in both healthy volunteers and people with mental health difficulties. We get so many emails from emails from prospective trainees that it has now become impossible to reply to them all. Please accept our apologies if you do not get a response, but hopefully all questions are covered by the below: </p>

<h2 style="text-align:left;">PhD students</h2>
<p style="text-align:left;">The only route to doing a PhD with us is through the centrally managed schemes:</p>

<ul style="text-align:left;">
  <li><a href="https://www.lido-dtp.ac.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">London Interdisciplinary Doctoral Programme (LIDo)</a></li>
  <li><a href="https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-conducted-at-nimh/scientific-director/office-of-fellowship-and-training/nimh-ucl-graduate-neuroscience-program/ucl-nimh-faq.shtml" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UCL-NIMH Joint Doctoral Training Program in Neuroscience</a> (US citizens and permanent residents)</li>
  <li><a href="https://www.uclbbk-mrcdtp.ac.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UCL-Birkbeck MRC DTP</a></li>
</ul>

<p style="text-align:left;">There is no need to contact us before applying to these programs as they are centrally managed. If you get onto them you can get in touch to do rotations in the lab. We do not take self-funded PhD students.</p>

<h2 style="text-align:left;">Postdoctoral researchers</h2>
<p style="text-align:left;">All postdoctoral positions will be advertised on the UCL website and jobs.ac.uk.We do also occasionally support independent fellowship applications to be hosted by the lab, but it should be noted that these have become absurdly competitive in recent years so should not be embarked on lightly.</p>

<h2 style="text-align:left;">Research assistants</h2>
<p style="text-align:left;">All RA positions will be advertised on the UCL website and jobs.ac.uk. Unlike some institutions we do not have discretionary funds for non advertised position </p>

<h2 style="text-align:left;">Interns</h2>
<p style="text-align:left;">We do not take unpaid interns out of equality and equity concerns. The only way to do an internship is to obtain paid funding. Oliver set up the BAP scheme for this purpose and we are happy to support one candidate for this scheme a year. For more details see here: https://www.bap.org.uk/internship.php. If you obtain other sources of stipend for an internship (e.g. via a scheme at your undergraduate university) then we are happy to consider taking you. Please get in touch. </p>

<h2 style="text-align:left;">Master's and undergraduate projects</h2>
<p style="text-align:left;">There is no need to contact us prior to getting onto a masters or undergrad course. Once you are on a suitable programme the process of applying for a research project will be explained. We have a competitive process for selecting trainees for projects in the lab as we are unfortunately not able to support everyone who contacts us. </p>

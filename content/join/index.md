<p style="text-align:left;">We receive so many emails from prospective trainees that it is no longer possible to reply to them all. Please accept our apologies if you do not get a response, but we have put together a list that answers the most common questions.</p>

<h2 style="text-align:left;">PhD students</h2>
<p style="text-align:left;">Generally, the only route to doing a PhD with us is through one of these three externally managed schemes:</p>

<ul style="text-align:left;">
  <li><a href="https://www.lido-dtp.ac.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">London Interdisciplinary Doctoral Programme (LIDo)</a></li>
  <li><a href="https://www.uclbbk-mrcdtp.ac.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UCL-Birkbeck MRC DTP</a></li>
  <li><a href="https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-conducted-at-nimh/scientific-director/office-of-fellowship-and-training/nimh-ucl-graduate-neuroscience-program/ucl-nimh-faq.shtml" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UCL-NIMH Joint Doctoral Training Program in Neuroscience</a> (US citizens and permanent residents)</li>
</ul>

<p style="text-align:left;">There is no need to contact us before applying to the LIDo or MRC programmes. If you get onto them, you can get in touch to do rotations in the lab. If you are a US citizen interested in the NIMH programme, please get in touch before applying, as we would need to identify a suitable NIMH mentor. Note, we do not take self-funded PhD students and we do not usually support students for the internal UCL funded schemes as they are generally unsucessful. The one exception is the new <a href="https://www.ucl.ac.uk/scholarships/ucl-research-opportunity-scholarship" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UCL Research Opportunity Scholarship
</a> for (UK home fee eligible) underrepresented minority students. Please get in touch if you are interested in this scheme.</p>

<h2 style="text-align:left;">Postdoctoral researchers</h2>
<p style="text-align:left;">All postdoctoral positions will be advertised on the <a href="https://www.ucl.ac.uk/work-at-ucl/search-ucl-jobs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UCL jobs page</a> and <a href="https://www.jobs.ac.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">jobs.ac.uk</a>. We do occasionally support independent fellowship applications to be hosted by the lab, but these have become extremely competitive in recent years, so they should not be embarked on lightly. Please get in touch if you are interested in, for example, Wellcome Trust, MRC, ERC or NIHR schemes. In general you will need to be at least a three years post-PhD with multiple first author papers/preprints to be competitive.</p>

<h2 style="text-align:left;">Research assistants</h2>
<p style="text-align:left;">All RA positions will be advertised on the <a href="https://www.ucl.ac.uk/work-at-ucl/search-ucl-jobs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UCL jobs page</a> and <a href="https://www.jobs.ac.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">jobs.ac.uk</a>. We do not have discretionary funds for unadvertised positions so are unlikely to respond to speculative emails about RA positions.</p>

<h2 style="text-align:left;">Interns</h2>
<p style="text-align:left;">We do not take unpaid interns, out of equality and equity concerns. The only way to do an internship in the lab is to obtain paid external funding. Oliver set up the <a href="https://www.bap.org.uk/internship.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">British Association for Psychopharmacology internship scheme</a> for this purpose, and we are happy to support one candidate for this scheme a year. If you obtain another source of stipend for an internship (for example, via a scheme at your undergraduate university), we are happy to consider taking you. Please get in touch once you have identified such a funding source.</p>

<h2 style="text-align:left;">Master's and undergraduate projects</h2>
<p style="text-align:left;">There is no need to contact us before getting onto a master's or undergraduate course. Once you are on a suitable programme, the process of applying for a research project will be explained. We have a competitive process for selecting trainees for projects in the lab, as we are unfortunately not able to support everyone who contacts us.</p>
